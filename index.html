SHIPMENT LIFECYCLE

Seven checkpoints, one continuous record

Every consignment moves through the same fixed sequence. Nothing skips a step, and nothing gets updated twice.

01

Manifest created

Shipment logged with sender, receiver, and weight on file.

02

Sorting hub allocation

Assigned to a hub for consolidation and onward routing.

03

Customs clearing

Documentation reviewed and cleared for border crossing.

04

In transit

Moving between hubs on its route to destination.

05

Port of discharge checkpoint

Unloaded and verified at the destination port.

06

Out for delivery

Loaded for final-mile delivery to the consignee.

07

Delivered

Confirmed received. Record closed and archived.
