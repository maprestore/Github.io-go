GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers for the questions that come up first.

Everything customers and operations teams need before a shipment moves.

Do customers need an account to track a shipment?

No. A valid tracking ID is enough to view the latest public shipment record.

What information is shown on a tracking record?

The record can include the current stage, sender, receiver, location, estimated arrival, and the latest operational note.

Can the operations team export shipment data?

Yes. Authorized operators can filter the ledger and export the current shipment view as CSV.
