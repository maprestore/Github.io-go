Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote, contact our routing desk, subscribe to updates, or use shipment tracking, we may process the information you submit and technical data needed to keep the service secure.

How we use it

We use information to provide logistics services, respond to enquiries, operate shipment visibility tools, improve reliability, and meet legal or customs obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Shipment and enquiry records are retained only for operational, accounting, and compliance needs. Access is restricted to authorised team members and protected with session controls, transport encryption, and service-provider safeguards.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information where applicable. Email support@logiswift.delivery with the subject “Privacy request”.

Cookies

LogiSwift uses essential browser storage for preferences such as recent shipment searches and cookie consent. Optional analytics or marketing cookies should only be enabled with your consent.
