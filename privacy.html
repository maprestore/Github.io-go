PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy

Last updated:

What we collect

To provide shipment tracking, we store the information needed to create and update a shipment record: sender and consignee names, origin and destination, shipment status, and timestamps for each stage of the delivery lifecycle.

Looking up a shipment on the public tracking page does not require an account and does not collect personal information beyond the shipment ID or name entered for the search.

How it's used

  • To display shipment status to the person tracking it
  • To let operators manage and update shipment records
  • To generate CSV exports for the operator's own recordkeeping

What we don't do

We do not sell shipment or consignee data to third parties, and we do not use tracking data for advertising purposes.

Data retention

Shipment records are retained for as long as the operator's account remains active, or as required for operational and recordkeeping purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the operator managing your shipment.
