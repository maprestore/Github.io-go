ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Built for the question every shipment gets asked at least once.

"Where is it right now?" Consignment Ops exists so that question has a fast, public answer — without a phone call, a support ticket, or a spreadsheet lookup.

What this is

Consignment Ops is a shipment tracking and operations record for freight forwarders, couriers, and logistics teams. Every shipment gets a fixed, seven-stage lifecycle — from manifest creation to final delivery — so status means the same thing to everyone looking at it: the customer, the operator, and the partner carrier.

There's a public lookup page for customers, and an operator dashboard for the team managing the actual freight. Both read from the same record, so there's never a mismatch between what a customer sees and what's actually true.

How it's built

The lookup and dashboard run on a straightforward stack: a Node.js/Express backend, a PostgreSQL database, and a frontend that doesn't depend on a heavy framework to load. No account is required to track a shipment — the barrier to checking status should be zero.

What it's built on

01

One fixed sequence

Six stages, always in the same order. No custom statuses that mean different things to different people.

02

No login to look up

A customer shouldn't need an account just to find out where their shipment is.

03

One record, two views

The public tracker and the operator dashboard read the same data — never two sources of truth.

04

Exportable, not locked in

Shipment records can be pulled out as CSV any time — the data belongs to the operator, not the platform.
